externals/NetSpell.SpellChecker, retaining their comments and attribution:
Copyright (c) 2003, Paul Welter. Modified by Henk Westhuis for GitExtensions and

setup/assets/Dictionaries. All existing [Copyright] sections and authorship and
licence statements are retained. They are not converted to Hunspell
format or relabelled as one common dictionary licence.
