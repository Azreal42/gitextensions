# Isolated font-stream regression

This is test infrastructure only. The product has no probe switch. The positive NUnit case `FontStreamLifetimeTests.RegularFontExportsActualSourceBytesUnderGcInAnIsolatedProcess` creates its own private fixture from the actual `fc-match` Regular result, then compares all 256 real exports with those installed-file bytes under bounded GC pressure. No assumed font family or embedded font is needed. The parent remains outside AvaloniaTest and never resolves a font.

Integration: import `../../../eng/linux/font-stream-regression/Desktop.Tests.fragment.targets` from Desktop.Tests.csproj. It builds the probe without referencing its executable assembly in the testhost, links only the plain contract source, and copies its entire output to `font-stream-probe/`. The current-build subtree must be included recursively in Rootfull's immutable runtime input manifest. The Green backend/native hashes must also match Desktop.Tests. Add the `fontconfig` CLI package to Linux CI prerequisites; absence or ambiguous output is an explicit failure, not a silent skip.

The project references the local Avalonia.Skia backend and neutralizes compile/runtime assets from the official package. The product repair remains only `using var asset`. No partial-read implementation or unsafe replacement exists here. Closure, loaded modules, source/copy/input hashes, raw stdout/stderr and actual parent waits are retained in the printed private fixture path. Outputs are capped at 512 KiB each; fc-match 5 s, probe 30 s, case 40 s; pressure 10 s / 20000 passes, Join 2 s, source 32 MiB. The 256 pressure exports copy at most 8 GiB; the preceding qualification export adds at most 32 MiB. GC overlap requires GC.CollectionCount(0) or GC.CollectionCount(GC.MaxGeneration) to increase directly across TryGetStream; worker passes are separate information. This does not locate a GC within memcpy. A timeout stop is targeted at the owned Process and always fails. A failed Join never counts as success.

For separate development calibration, retain one qualified fixture, create two fresh copies of the complete probe output, put the official versus repaired Avalonia.Skia only in their corresponding closure, and regenerate the input's binary pins/backend/native hashes for each. Keep the same actual source bytes, source identity and fixed stress protocol. Invoke the runtime directly with `FontStreamRegression.dll ABSOLUTE_INPUT_JSON ABSOLUTE_FRESH_RECEIPT_JSON` through Root's own parent/wait recorder. The probe always expects successful byte exports; there is no “expected crash means pass” mode. Two planned official and two repaired children give bounded comparative evidence. Any official survivor is preserved; no targeted native crash under observed GC means `ROUGE_NON_REPRODUIT`. This calibration is never part of an ordinary full run.

Read the receipt and raw waits separately. Raw exit 139 alone is not a signal classification. A matching native stack is a separate witness. A byte-normalized/TTC export or mismatch between fontconfig and public resolution fails preconditions rather than widening the product patch. Regular does not prove synthetic styles, every family, UI parity, PerTest teardown or the cause of crash 05. Reflection only binds the real version-linked TryGetStream; no internal locator, method rewriting or GC suppression is used.

The 40 s case cancellation is separate from bounded failure cleanup: after an owned stop, wait up to 3 s; drain up to 3 s, then cancel and observe both readers for up to 2 s. The original error and streamsDrained=false are retained even if cancellation retires the readers. Non-retirement is explicitly recorded, and late reader faults are observed; cleanup never turns failure into success.

No restore, build, test, fc-match or probe has been executed by the author before Root's freeze/GO.
